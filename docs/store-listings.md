## en — English
**Name:** Health Universe
**Subtitle:** Private health measurement journal
**Description:** Record and review weight, pulse, blood pressure, water intake and sleep in a personal journal on your device. Manage your entries and view your history. Not a medical device; not for diagnosis or emergencies.
